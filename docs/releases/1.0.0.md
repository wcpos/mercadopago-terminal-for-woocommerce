# Mercado Pago Terminal for WooCommerce 1.0.0

This is the version of the Mercado Pago terminal extension for WCPOS 2.0. It needs WooCommerce POS Pro 2.0 or newer; without it, the extension shows a notice and takes no payments. After updating, register the new webhook URL in Mercado Pago and paste its secret signature: notifications without a valid signature are now refused.

## Changed

- Payments now run through WooCommerce POS Pro. The payment panel on the order-pay page, terminal choice, the background check for finished payments and the support bundle all come from Pro.
- Terminals are chosen in the WooCommerce POS settings. The old terminal settings in this extension are no longer used.
- Webhooks go to a new address, `/wp-json/wcpos/v2/payments/webhook?provider=mercadopago`, and must be signed. A missing secret or a wrong signature is refused.
- Payments still open when you update are taken over without sending a second order to the terminal. Old order data is kept.
- Sales taken with earlier versions can still be refunded, in full or in part, from the WooCommerce order screen.
- A refund made on the terminal or in Mercado Pago is not added to the WooCommerce order. Record it in WooCommerce yourself.
- The extension's own log settings and support bundle download are gone. Logs are in WooCommerce's logs under the source `mercadopago-terminal`, and the support bundle is the **Support bundle** row Pro adds to the settings page.
- The fixes from 0.1.1 are kept: shops with 0 decimals send whole-unit amounts, an order Mercado Pago reports in another currency is not completed, and Mercado Pago's error details appear in error messages.

## Upgrade

Update WooCommerce POS Pro to 2.0 or newer, then this plugin. Open **WooCommerce → Settings → Payments → Mercado Pago Terminal**. The new webhook URL is shown under **Webhook secret**. In Mercado Pago, under Your integrations → Webhooks, replace the old URL with it, choose the "Order (Mercado Pago)" event, and paste the secret signature into **Webhook secret**. Then choose your terminals in the WooCommerce POS settings.

The **Mode** setting (Test or Live) only labels the access token you paste. It does not switch to another token. Finish any open payments before you replace the token. Your store currency must match the currency of your Mercado Pago account. If you changed the order sent to Mercado Pago with the `mptfwc_order_payload` filter, that filter has been removed.

## Not yet tested

This release is checked by automated tests only. It has not been run against a Mercado Pago account, the Mercado Pago sandbox or a Point terminal. Try a payment and a refund on your own account before relying on it.
